# Game layouts

These layouts follow the saved Cyberpunk arrangement: WASD on G4/G10/G11/G12, Escape on G1, nearby Q/E and F/R where useful, and Space on G22. The mouse remains the aiming device. Thumb buttons provide game actions. Blank keys are intentional.

Cyberpunk is preserved exactly, including duplicate R/X/V bindings and its 60% brightness in the exported and first-run profile. It may assume custom in-game bindings. Fresh installs start with Cyberpunk on M1; existing saved profiles are not replaced.

Apply a **Starter template**, inspect it, then **Save**. Templates replace all key and thumb bindings and macros and select directional-stick mode; they retain brightness, LCD media, and M-slot assignment.

## DOOM (2016)

Combat keeps glory kill beside D, chainsaw on G17, equipment on G19 and a thumb button, and jump on G22. Q retains access to the full weapon wheel; only three direct weapon slots are included. Shift is walk, not sprint. R switches weapon mods; there is no reload binding.

Vertical movement uses G18 to descend and G22 to ascend. The five starting tool slots remain accessible, while F/Q/R and the mouse operate tools. Shift activates a biomod. Later unlocked slots 6–10 remain on the keyboard. Early-access controls can change; check the in-game Controls menu.

## The Ascent

Q/E activate the two augmentations; G15 holds crouch and G22 evades. G17 and the lower thumb button fire the tactical, while G18 and the left thumb button hold the cyberdeck. Weapon swapping remains on the mouse wheel; this driver does not map wheel scrolling. Spare keys stay empty instead of emitting unrelated actions.

## X4: Foundations

WASD is ship strafing, with Q/E roll and stick arrows for steering. G16/G18 form the speed decrease/increase pair, G20 stops engines, G15 holds boost, and G22 fires. Travel/scan modes, docking, and autopilot use balanced one-shot chords. SETA, quicksave, and other less frequent controls remain on the keyboard. Flight controls take priority over matching shooter action positions.
